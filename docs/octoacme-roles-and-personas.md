# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads shape the end-user experience and ensure product decisions are grounded in user needs, accessibility, and usability.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research, journey mapping, and usability validation
- Translate customer needs into wireframes, prototypes, and design requirements
- Partner with Product Managers on prioritization and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with Developers and QA to ensure design intent is implemented and testable
- Identify accessibility, usability, and content gaps before release

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to align user outcomes with business goals
- Support Developers by clarifying interaction flows and edge cases
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate usability, accessibility, and acceptance criteria
- Inform Project Managers of design dependencies, risks, and timeline impacts

### Goals
- Improve usability, trust, and adoption
- Reduce rework caused by unclear product expectations
- Support a consistent, accessible user experience

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects provide technical direction, help resolve design trade-offs, and keep implementations aligned with long-term maintainability and platform standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, technical standards, and integration patterns
- Review technical design and implementation choices with Developers
- Identify and escalate major technical risks, dependencies, and constraints
- Support planning by estimating complexity and cross-system impacts
- Help ensure maintainability, security, and operational readiness

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partner with Project Managers to surface dependencies, risk, and delivery impacts
- Work with Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, timing, and technical feasibility
- Mentor Developers and contribute to code review quality and design consistency
- Coordinate with Security and Release/Operations Leads on technical controls and deployment readiness

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions
- Reduce avoidable technical debt and cross-team friction
- Keep the implementation aligned with project goals and architecture principles

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how success, customer behavior, and operational health are measured so product decisions are grounded in evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans, dashboards, and success metrics
- Recommend instrumentation for product usage, reliability, and business impact
- Validate reporting accuracy and data quality with engineering and QA
- Partner with Product Managers on customer and business outcome analysis
- Support retrospective and continuous improvement by clarifying what changed and why

### How they interact with existing roles
- Align with Product Managers on KPIs, targets, and the meaning of success
- Partner with Developers to ensure tracking and event definitions are implemented correctly
- Work with QA/Testing to validate reporting and data integrity in release checks
- Inform Project Managers of measurement dependencies and reporting needs

### Goals
- Improve decision-making through measurable outcomes
- Detect issues early with actionable business and operational signals
- Connect delivery progress to customer and business value

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads ensure the project follows required controls, handles sensitive data appropriately, and reduces risk before and during release.

### Responsibilities
- Review solutions for security, privacy, and compliance implications
- Define required controls, risk mitigations, and review checkpoints
- Partner with Developers and QA to validate secure implementation and testing
- Escalate emerging risks, exposures, and approval needs to leadership
- Support release readiness with security evidence and remediation tracking

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Developers to review architecture, authentication, data handling, and dependencies
- Coordinate with Project Managers on risk communication, status reporting, and mitigation timing
- Support Product Managers by clarifying business and customer risk trade-offs
- Partner with Release/Operations Leads to ensure security gates are met before deployment

### Goals
- Reduce product and customer risk
- Improve compliance and operational trust
- Ensure security considerations are built into delivery decisions

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads manage deployment readiness, monitoring, and operational continuity so changes are safe to ship and easy to support.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, deployment readiness, rollback plans, and monitoring
- Validate production and staging readiness with QA and engineering partners
- Develop operational handoff plans and support documentation
- Partner with stakeholders on launch communications and incident response readiness
- Track production health after rollout and support immediate mitigation work

### How they interact with existing roles
- Collaborate with Developers and QA/Testing on smoke tests, release checks, and validation gates
- Inform Project Managers of launch risks, timing, and stakeholder communications
- Work with Product Managers on release messaging and customer impact expectations
- Coordinate with Security / Privacy Leads to confirm controls and contingency plans are in place

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and operational disruption
- Improve deployment confidence and reliability
- Enable faster, safer recovery when issues occur

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Representatives bring customer feedback, operational reality, and post-release learnings into the planning and delivery cycle.

### Responsibilities
- Capture recurring customer pain points, support trends, and escalation patterns
- Share feedback that informs backlog priorities and acceptance criteria
- Help define rollout communication needs and customer readiness guidance
- Partner with project stakeholders on adoption, confidence, and support planning
- Provide post-release feedback to improve product quality and customer experience

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Product Managers to prioritize issues that impact customer satisfaction or retention
- Support Project Managers with stakeholder communications and release readiness updates
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing on real-world failure modes, edge cases, and supportability needs
- Inform the broader team on enabling issues, customer expectations, and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce avoidable support burden and rework
- Ensure product delivery stays connected to real customer needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- In smaller teams, one person may hold more than one role; in larger teams, these responsibilities may be split across multiple people.
